# LadiBDS Visual Master Rule

- Landing page visual master: the approved 728×2048 screenshot supplied by the client.
- Do not redesign, reorder, or replace the landing layout from the PDF.
- The uploaded Sun Galaxy training PDF is the authoritative source for factual copy, product quantities, floor plans, tower/shop/penthouse imagery, and post-click floor-plan states.
- Visual changes must preserve the approved landing composition. PDF-derived assets are fitted without distortion/cropping that changes their intended content.
